The controls that make it credible

  • The removed member’s session still worked; login failure did not explain the denial.
  • The owner could still read the document; a broken service did not explain the denial.
  • Cross-app accounts and outsiders were denied in both apps.
  • Re-grants and alternate grants preserved legitimate access.
  • The bounded defect was reset, followed by denial and owner checks.

Restoration was observed. The case remained open in this run: no human analyst decision or independent remediation approval is claimed.
