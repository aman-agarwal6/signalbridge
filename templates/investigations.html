Understand the detection rules

R1 · Repeated private-resource access failures

Detects failed private reads across at least three distinct resources within a rolling five-minute window. Cases group qualifying window endpoints into fixed UTC buckets. Repeated attempts on one resource alone do not meet the threshold.

R2 · Access after a recorded revocation

Flags an allowed private-resource read whose source reports revocation or a policy regression. The rule does not independently prove that revocation occurred. Severity indicates review priority, not a confirmed breach.
