These are results on 22 selected AI/builder-authored scenarios. They are not production accuracy estimates, independent validation or a comparison with enterprise products. Historical alerts corrected by late evidence remain visible and are reported separately.

The engineering change

R3 joins a separate resource membership removal to a later allowed read. A new closed event schema identifies the affected account; a server-controlled credential permission limits who can send that assertion. Matching stays within one app, environment, evidence source and resource, over 24 hours. Re-grants and ambiguous timing matter.

Existing integrations still send their earlier event format. The new behavior is verified through in-process signed requests; a native source adapter for these richer membership events has not been enabled.

E01 · Detected

Investigate a possible permission failure

A separate removal assertion at t=0 identifies the affected account and exact resource. At t=7 the same account has an allowed read labeled member. R3 links both observations; it does not need the read to call itself suspicious.

Analyst decision. High review priority is justified by the potential private-data exposure. The fixture's oracle says content was returned; the telemetry itself only says allowed. Check actual returned content, alternate permissions, clock accuracy, missing grants and a legitimate owner control before calling this a confirmed disclosure.

Remediation & verification. If the source check is defective, enforce current resource membership at the read boundary. Retest the unchanged removed-member session, legitimate owner, denied/error distinction and restoration. That source-app repair was not performed by this evaluation.

E11 · No alert

Explain legitimate access after a re-grant

Removal occurs at t=0, an authorized grant at t=2, and an allowed read at t=7. The latest available membership state permits access. No R3 case is opened.

Analyst decision. Classify this controlled case as expected activity after verifying that the grant was authorized. A prior removal alone is insufficient evidence of a current violation. No alert does not mean that every permission check is healthy.

Remediation & verification. No permission fix is warranted from this sequence. Preserve the timeline and test another removal after the grant; E03 exercises that sequence and should trigger review.

E16 · False alert

Investigate a false alert instead of hiding it

A legitimate user follows three stale bookmarks within 40 seconds. The three distinct not-visible resources meet R1, producing a medium case even though the declared scenario is benign.

Analyst decision. This is one of the two measured false alerts. Confirm resource existence, expected access and why the links are stale before a false-positive decision. Do not broadly suppress managed devices or all not-visible results.

Remediation & verification. A proposed tuning change needs positive and negative controls, another-person review and a new evaluation round. Keep this receipt unchanged. E17 adds another false alert caused by a missing grant; detection alone cannot reconstruct that absent fact.

E18 · No alert

Correct an alert when delayed evidence arrives

The collector first sees removal t=0 and read t=7. It opens R3. A grant effective at t=3 arrives last. The existing case is retained, changed to medium priority and reopened for reassessment, with a new evidence version.

Analyst decision. Distinguish event time from arrival order. The final label is benign because access was restored before the read. The initial alert still consumed analyst attention and is reported separately from final-state false-positive counts.

E19 · Inconclusive

Keep a service outage inconclusive

After a removal, the read produces dependency_unavailable with outcome error. No successful read or explicit denial is established; R3 does not alert.

Analyst decision. Record the authorization outcome as inconclusive. Investigate service health and repeat the check. Treating an error as proof of safe denial would turn missing evidence into false assurance.

Remediation & verification. Restore only the authorized lab dependency, then repeat member, removed-member and owner controls with known private content. This evaluation injects metadata; it does not recover or test a real service.
